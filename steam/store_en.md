## App name
GAFIL

## Short description (max 300 characters)
A tiny pixel cat that lives on your desktop, climbs your windows, and answers your questions with Claude. Commands and screen actions always ask first. Simple math and conversions are answered right on your PC. Requires Claude Code and your own Anthropic API key.

## About This Game
```
[h2]A desk cat that actually helps[/h2]
GAFIL is a small pixel cat that wanders along the bottom of your screen. Click it, ask anything, and it answers using Anthropic's Claude — from quick questions to real work on your PC.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Chat with Claude[/b] — answers stream into a little speech bubble. Press Ctrl+Alt+G anywhere to call it.
[*][b]Real work, with your approval[/b] — run commands, tidy folders, edit files. Every command and file change shows an approval window first.
[*][b]Screen control[/b] — for tasks that need the screen. Asks once per task, shows a red border, and stops the moment you move your mouse.
[*][b]Quick answers, zero tokens[/b] — math, percentages, unit conversions, world clocks and D-days are worked out on your PC without calling Claude.
[*][b]Reminders[/b] — "remind me in 30 minutes" and the cat runs to the middle of the screen when it's time.
[*][b]Drop files on it[/b] — drag a file or some text onto the cat and ask for a summary.
[/list]

[h2]And it's a cat[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hops onto your windows, climbs their sides, naps on the edge with its tail hanging down.
[*]Sits in small new windows like they're boxes. Grooms after you carry it by the scruff. Kneads when things are quiet.
[*]Body-language alerts: sits on error dialogs, taps frozen apps, and flops over when you've worked too long.
[*]Pants when your CPU is busy, sweats when your GPU is hot, dozes at night.
[*]5 fur colors, 4 outfits, and a few secrets it will tell you once you're close.
[/list]

[h2]Light on your PC[/h2]
Simple pixel art, no heavy rendering. When you haven't asked anything for 10 minutes, it shuts down the Claude process to give back memory — handy while gaming.

[h2]Before you start[/h2]
[list]
[*]GAFIL needs [b]Claude Code[/b] (Anthropic's free command-line tool). On first launch GAFIL can install it for you with one click.
[*]GAFIL connects with [b]your own Anthropic API key[/b]. Claude usage is billed by Anthropic to your account; GAFIL itself is free and takes no cut.
[*]What you ask, files you share with it, command results and screenshots during screen control are sent to Anthropic, as when using Claude Code.
[/list]

GAFIL is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. "Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.
```
